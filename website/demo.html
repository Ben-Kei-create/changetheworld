Hana Nakamura · 72 · Retired Nurse · Tokyo, Japan

Real World Context

In Japan, 1 in 3 people over 65 live alone. Studies show that social isolation is linked to a 26% increase in the risk of premature death — equivalent to smoking 15 cigarettes a day. The Tokyo metropolitan government launched a "loneliness task force" in 2020 after studies found 40% of elderly residents had no meaningful social contact.

Real World Context

Grief and loneliness are deeply intertwined. Research by Cacioppo & Patrick shows that feeling socially connected — even in silence, in the presence of another — reduces cortisol levels and activates the same neural pathways as physical safety. A space doesn't need words to be healing.
