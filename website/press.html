TERRA is a Mac narrative game that puts players inside the real decisions facing humanity — told through seven characters across six continents, grounded in peer-reviewed data and lived experience.

About the Game

TERRA is not a game about saving the world. It is a game about understanding it.

Each of the seven playable stories is built from primary sources: IPCC reports, UNHCR field data, peer-reviewed psychology research, UN environment assessments. Players do not fight. They listen, choose, and carry the weight of what they learn.

The experience ends with a question the player answers for themselves — and an echo of something they wrote at the very beginning, before the first story. No one else sees either answer.

"Understanding is the first step to change.
We don't ask you to save the world.
We ask you to understand it."

The Seven Stories

Amara Diallo 28 · Senegal · Solar Engineer Climate Crisis — Africa contributes <4% of global CO₂ yet bears the greatest climate burden. Amara has 48 hours to stop a bribe from killing 40 villages' access to solar power.
Mei Lin 19 · Shanghai · Researcher Ocean Pollution — 170 million metric tons of plastic in the world's oceans. Mei has mapped the data. Her professor wants her to soften the conclusions.
Carlos Mendoza 45 · Amazon · Community Leader Deforestation — 10 million hectares of forest lost every year. Carlos must decide whether to accept a deal that would protect his community but cost the forest around it.
Fatima Al-Rashid 17 · Jordan · Refugee Student Inequality & Education — 224 million children out of school. Fatima has a scholarship application and three days before the camp generator fails permanently.
James Okafor 52 · Lagos · Urban Farmer Urban Poverty — 690 million people go hungry each day. James's rooftop garden feeds 23 families. The city has issued him an eviction notice.
Hana Yoshida 72 · Tokyo · Former Teacher Human Isolation — loneliness is a health risk equivalent to smoking 15 cigarettes a day. Hana has not spoken to anyone in eleven days.
Astrid Lindqvist 16 · Malmö · Student Climate Anxiety — 59% of young people globally are very or extremely worried about climate change. Astrid is asked to speak at a conference. She doesn't know what to say.

Research Foundation

Every story is built from primary and peer-reviewed sources:

  • IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (2021–2022)
  • IEA Africa Energy Outlook 2022
  • Jambeck et al. (2015), Science — plastic ocean inputs
  • UNEP: From Pollution to Solution (2021)
  • Rights and Resources Initiative: Who Owns the World's Land?
  • UNHCR Education Report 2022: The Right to Learn
  • UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report
  • FAO: Urban Food Systems and COVID-19 Response
  • Holt-Lunstad, J. (2015) — loneliness as health risk, Perspectives on Psychological Science
  • Hickman et al. (2021) — climate anxiety in young people, The Lancet Planetary Health
  • SMHI: Baltic Sea temperature records, 1960–2025

Full attribution is available in the in-game Credits screen.

Design & Approach

TERRA is built for macOS 14+ as a native SwiftUI application. No ads. No network calls. No analytics. No data leaves the device — ever. The privacy policy is four sentences long.

Accessibility is built into the core, not added after: variable text sizing, reduced motion mode, and high-contrast text are all available from settings. Reading time estimates appear before each story begins.

The game is available in English and Japanese.
