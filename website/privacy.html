PRIVACY POLICY

We don't collect your data.
Full stop.

TERRA does not collect, transmit, share, or store any personal information.

Your game progress — which stories you've completed, what choices you made, your impact score — is stored locally on your Mac using Apple's standard UserDefaults system. It never leaves your device.

We have no analytics. No crash reporting that sends data to us. No ad identifiers. No third-party SDKs. No network requests at all.

If you delete TERRA from your Mac, everything is gone. We have nothing. That is intentional.
