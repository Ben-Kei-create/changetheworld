## Prepared by: Hiro Matsumoto, Marketing Lead

## Executive Summary

TERRA is positioned as a **premium narrative experience** in the Mac App Store.
Our target: thoughtful adults (25–55) who feel the weight of the world but don't know where to start.
Price: **$19.99 USD** — premium, no IAP, no subscriptions.

| Segment | Profile | Why TERRA |

## App Store Listing

### Name
**TERRA — Stories of Our World**

### Subtitle (30 chars max)
`Six lives. One planet. Your choice.`

### Description (4000 chars, key sections)

**Opening hook:**
```
In Senegal, a solar engineer faces a bribe that could power 40 villages — or compromise everything.
In China, a 19-year-old researcher holds data that corporations want buried.
In the Amazon, an indigenous land defender has 72 hours to save a forest his family has protected for generations.

These are not hypotheticals. These are the decisions humanity faces, right now.

TERRA puts you inside them.
```

**Feature highlights:**
- 6 original stories, grounded in real-world research and data
- Branching narrative — every choice has weight, and consequences
- No combat, no timers, no lives. Just stories.
- Real-world impact data after every chapter
- Stunning minimalist visuals and original atmospheric score
- Play at your pace. Save anytime.

**Closing:**
```
TERRA was built on one belief: understanding is the first step to change.
We don't ask you to save the world. We ask you to understand it.
$19.99. One-time purchase. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases.
Your purchase supports the development of new stories.
```

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Pre-launch (6 weeks out)
- Press kit to 20 Mac/indie game journalists: Mac Stories, 9to5Mac, Touch Arcade Mac
- 3 "making of" posts (character research, design philosophy, real-world partnerships)
- App Store pre-order page (30-day countdown)

### Phase 2: Launch Week
- Product Hunt launch (Tuesday morning, coordinated upvotes)
- Press embargo lift — target: at least 2 reviews live at launch
- Twitter/X thread: "the real stories behind TERRA's characters" (Emma Thompson advising)
- Launch trailer (60 sec, no voice-over, music + text only)

### Phase 3: Post-launch
- "New Story" DLC drops every quarter ($4.99 each or free for early buyers TBD)
- Educator license program ($199/classroom/year, unlimited installs)
- Localization: Japanese (built), French, Spanish, Arabic (Q3)

## Pricing Rationale

$19.99 is the **deliberate** price. Not $9.99. Not $14.99. Not $24.99.

- Below the psychological "this had better be perfect" threshold of $25
- Above the "casual app" perception threshold of $10
- Comparable to: Monument Valley 2, Alto's Odyssey, mini metro — all $5–10
- But TERRA is longer, deeper, more serious — premium positioning justified
- One-time purchase signals: **we respect you and your wallet**
